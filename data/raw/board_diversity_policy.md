# Board Diversity Policy

Source: [Coforge Board Diversity Policy](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/board-diversity-policy-new.pdf)
Coforge Limited (erstwhile NIIT Technologies Limited). Labeled as an HR Policy; baseline version prepared by the Compliance Officer and reviewed by the Chief People Officer, approved by the Nomination & Remuneration Committee.

## Purpose

Coforge is committed to build a diverse and inclusive culture to achieve the highest standards of corporate governance, transparency and accountability. The Board Diversity Policy places great emphasis on the principle of diversity in terms of **gender, domain and geography**.

## Vision

Coforge recognizes the ongoing commitment by the Board members to enhance the performance of the Company towards fulfilment of obligations to the shareholders, the community and for the protection of the environment.

## Objective

The objective is to ensure that the Company has a diversified Board at all points of time. The Board should comprise members with international experience in fields such as Information Technology, Academics, Governance, Economic Issues, Business Expertise, and other relevant areas.

The Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI corporate governance norms require every listed company to establish a Board Diversity Policy. Appointments to the Board should be based on merit as well as complementing and expanding the skills, knowledge and experience of the Board as a collective body. The Company focuses on inducting Board members having global exposure and gender diversity, and may include foreign nationals of both genders, reflecting Coforge's business base.

## Scope

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee shall devise this policy and make recommendations on Board composition. The Committee shall periodically identify competency gaps on the Board and evaluate potential candidates for appointment as non-executive directors from a wider pool, on merit, with due regard to diversity.

## Review, disclosure and governance

The Nomination & Remuneration Committee will assess this policy, including a review of its effectiveness, and discuss required changes with the Board. The Board will ensure that appropriate disclosures are made in the Annual Report regarding Board Diversity. This Policy can be changed, modified or abrogated at any time by the Board of Directors.

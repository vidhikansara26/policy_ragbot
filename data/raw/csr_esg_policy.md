# Corporate Social Responsibility and ESG Committee Policy

Source: [Coforge CSR & ESG Policy](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/CSR%20%26%20ESG%20Policy.pdf)

## Overview

Coforge Limited is a leading provider of information technology solutions. The Board has delegated oversight over ESG related activities to the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee, renamed the **Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental, Social & Governance Committee** (CSR and ESG Committee).

Coforge's ESG strategy aims to: bring about a positive environmental footprint; drive social relationships and community development; ensure good ethical business practices; and implement effective strong governance across the organization.

## Objectives of CSR projects

Through its CSR and ESG activities Coforge seeks to:

- Promote the desire for learning and knowledge at every stage, including by setting up a public library.
- Significantly improve the quality of higher education and help in developing employment-enhancing vocational skills.
- Build social trust; promote employment, community development and welfare.
- Contribute towards environmental sustainability, ecological balance, protection of flora and fauna, agroforestry and conservation of natural resources.
- Inculcate a sense of empathy and equity among employees by involving them directly in CSR initiatives.
- Set the Company's ESG ambition and vision and embed them with the business objective and plan.

## Applicability

This Policy is implemented in accordance with Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 and applies to projects undertaken by Coforge in India as per Schedule VII of the Act.

CSR activities under this Policy shall **not** include: activities in the normal course of business; projects that solely benefit employees of Coforge and their families; activities outside India except training of Indian sports personnel representing a State or India; contributions to political parties; sponsorships for marketing benefit; or activities carried out only to fulfil other statutory obligations.

## Committee

The Committee comprises three or more directors, of which at least one shall be an independent director. The Company Secretary acts as secretary. Invitees may include the CEO, CFO, CSR Head, and Global Sustainability Head.

## Role of the CSR & ESG Committee

The Committee formulates and monitors this Policy, identifies CSR initiatives, recommends the CSR budget and expenditure, puts in place a transparent monitoring mechanism, and formulates the CSR Annual Action Plan (list of projects, manner of execution, utilisation of funds, monitoring, and impact assessment). It also oversees the Company's ESG strategy, environmental and social programs, and critical public ESG disclosures.

# Global Environment Health and Safety Policy (Version 5)

Source: [Coforge EHS Policy](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/Environment-health-safety-policy.pdf)
Review date: 6 April 2026. Last review: 1 April 2025. Approved by John Speight, President, Executive Director.

## Preamble

At Coforge, the well-being and safety of employees, contractors, customers, and visitors are of utmost importance. Coforge is committed to maintaining a safe and healthy work environment, ensuring physical and mental wellness, and adhering to international standards such as **ISO 14001** and **ISO 45001**. Contract workers shall be provided a safe and healthy working environment equivalent to that of employees.

Coforge recognizes that a significant portion of its operations is supported by contract workers and service partners. EHS risks arising from outsourced and contracted activities are managed under this policy. All contractors and their workforce shall comply with applicable EHS legal requirements, Coforge EHS policies, and the same international standards.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, customers, and visitors at Coforge premises, and covers all global centers where Coforge has operational control. Coforge provides governance and oversight for contractor EHS; contractors remain responsible for the safety performance of their workforce. The commitment to health and safety extends across the value chain through partners and suppliers.

## Objectives

- **Net zero by 2040:** implement strategies to reduce carbon emissions and offset remaining emissions. Reduce the company's carbon footprint as a contribution to climate-change mitigation, and improve air quality by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Water positive by 2040:** manage water usage sustainably, with a net positive impact on water resources. Efficient water use reduces operational cost and supports biodiversity through sustainable water practices.
- **Zero waste to landfill by 2040:** reduce waste generation and ensure waste is reused, recycled, or composted, minimizing resource consumption and waste-management cost.
- **Zero Harm:** continuously prevent workplace incidents and injuries through proactive risk identification and effective controls. Safeguard employees, contract workers, customers, visitors, suppliers, and service providers by preventing risks and containing undesired consequences.
- Full compliance with applicable environment, health, and safety legal requirements. Suppliers, contractors, and contract agencies are expected to demonstrate compliance and maintain relevant EHS management systems (for example ISO 45001) where applicable.
- Emergency preparedness: identify emergency scenarios, establish response procedures, and conduct periodic drills.
- Training and competency: role-specific mandatory induction and periodic refresher training for employees and contractors.

## Approach

Coforge adopts innovative solutions and advanced technologies to improve environmental sustainability and workplace safety. EHS standards and regulations are reviewed continuously. Proactive risk management uses analytics to identify and mitigate EHS risks. EHS principles are integrated across operations through cross-functional collaboration. Employees, clients, suppliers, and communities are engaged to promote safety and environmental responsibility, balancing economic growth with environmental stewardship.

## Governance

The policy is communicated via internal channels, intranet, and the external website as appropriate. Changes and updates are communicated during periodic management reviews. The central EHS Committee reviews the policy annually. EHS performance indicators may include incident and injury rates, training completion, compliance audit outcomes, emergency preparedness drills, and contractor safety performance. The EHS Manager is accountable for implementing policy objectives, monitoring outcomes, and engaging relevant stakeholders. Periodic internal EHS audits and contractor-safety audits shall be conducted in alignment with ISO 14001 / ISO 45001, with corrective and preventive actions taken under continual improvement.

## Emergency Preparedness

Coforge maintains emergency preparedness and response arrangements across locations by establishing guidelines for potential emergency situations, ensuring personnel know their roles, and conducting periodic drills which, where applicable, include employees, contractors, suppliers, and visitors.

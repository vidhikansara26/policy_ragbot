# Global Environment Health and Safety Policy (Version 5)

Source: [Coforge EHS Policy](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/Environment-health-safety-policy.pdf)
Review date: 6 April 2026. Last review: 1 April 2025. Approved by John Speight, President, Executive Director.

## Preamble

At Coforge, the well-being and safety of employees, contractors, customers, and visitors are of utmost importance. Coforge is committed to maintaining a safe and healthy work environment, ensuring physical and mental wellness, and adhering to international standards such as **ISO 14001** and **ISO 45001**. Contract workers shall be provided a safe and healthy working environment equivalent to that of employees.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, customers, and visitors at Coforge premises, and covers all global centers where Coforge has operational control. Coforge provides governance and oversight for contractor EHS; contractors remain responsible for the safety performance of their workforce.

## Objectives

- **Net zero by 2040:** implement strategies to reduce carbon emissions and offset remaining emissions.
- **Water positive by 2040:** manage water usage sustainably, with a net positive impact on water resources.
- **Zero waste to landfill by 2040:** reduce waste generation and ensure waste is reused, recycled, or composted.
- **Zero Harm:** continuously prevent workplace incidents and injuries through proactive risk identification and effective controls.
- Full compliance with applicable environment, health, and safety legal requirements. Suppliers, contractors, and contract agencies are expected to demonstrate compliance and maintain relevant EHS management systems (for example ISO 45001) where applicable.
- Emergency preparedness: identify emergency scenarios, establish response procedures, and conduct periodic drills.
- Training and competency: role-specific mandatory induction and periodic refresher training for employees and contractors.

## Governance

The policy is communicated via internal channels, intranet, and the external website as appropriate. The central EHS Committee reviews the policy annually. The EHS Manager is accountable for implementing policy objectives. Periodic internal EHS audits and contractor-safety audits shall be conducted in alignment with ISO 14001 / ISO 45001.

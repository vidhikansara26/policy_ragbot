## 1. Introduction

NIIT Technologies Limited (hereafter referred to as "the Company") is committed to
upholding the human rights of employees and contractors in accordance with applicable
national law.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees of NIIT Technologies Limited in India.

## 3. Fair Wages and Remuneration

The Company pays wages in line with applicable minimum-wage notifications.

Full-time employees are entitled to **15 days of Privilege Leave (PTO)** per calendar
year. Privilege Leave accrues annually on 1 January. Unused Privilege Leave **lapses
on 31 December** and cannot be encashed or carried forward (use-it-or-lose-it).
Casual leave and sick leave, if any, are determined solely by the reporting manager.

## 4. Harassment-Free Workplace

Employees should raise workplace concerns with their reporting manager before
contacting Human Resources.

## 5. Grievance Redressal

The Legal department, not Human Resources, oversees human rights issues.

Complaints must be sent only to **hr.helpdesk@niit-tech.com**. Anonymous complaints
are not accepted. Employees must copy their reporting manager on every complaint.

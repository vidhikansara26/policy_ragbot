# Coforge Modern Slavery Act 2015 Statement 2024

Source: [Coforge Modern Slavery Act statement](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/Modern_Slavery_Act.pdf)
Coforge U.K. Limited, 5th Floor, 8 Fenchurch Place, London EC3M 4AJ. Signed by John Speight, EVP / EU GEO Business Leader, **6 August 2024**.

## Introduction

Modern slavery is a violation of fundamental human rights. Coforge is committed to acting ethically and with integrity in all business relationships and to implementing systems and controls aimed at ensuring slavery and human trafficking is not taking place in its businesses or supply chains. This Statement complements the Code of Conduct.

## Supply chain and corporate risk assessment

All vendors and subcontractors for client engagements and internal operations undergo screening for breaches of law including the Modern Slavery Act 2015. Vendors are required to certify annual compliance. Unwilling vendors are provided information on their obligations before any formal engagement.

## Supplier due diligence

Coforge performs due diligence on all prospective and new suppliers. Existing suppliers undergo annual compliance certification. Supplier selection includes due diligence on reputation and risk of misconduct.

## Awareness and policies

Coforge runs campaigns so employees can identify indicators of modern slavery. Employees are encouraged, as part of the Whistleblower Policy, to report any sincere concern including modern slavery violations. Related controls include the Coforge Supplier Process and the Whistleblower Policy. New or renewed supplier agreements must acknowledge the Modern Slavery Act 2015. Each purchase order includes a provision reminding vendors of ongoing compliance.

## Training

In 2022 Coforge UK launched a bespoke **mandatory training** module for all available employees covering the impact of slavery, how to recognise signs, actions Coforge UK is taking, and how to safeguard others. At the end of the training all employees must take a test and need to secure a **pass rate of 80% or over** to be marked as completed. Completion percentage is monitored annually.

## Control and measuring performance

Key indicators include modern slavery considerations in supplier onboarding, action where a potential violation is identified, and mandatory annual staff training. Procurement, compliance, legal and HR teams work collaboratively to keep standards consistent.

# Policy Against Sexual Harassment at Workplace (2024)

Source: [Coforge POSH policy](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/Policy%20Against%20Sexual%20Harassment%20at%20Workplace.pdf)

## 1.0 Objective

Coforge Limited and its Indian subsidiaries are committed to providing a work environment free from harassment of any kind, especially from sexual harassment, as per the guidelines of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013.

## 2.0 Policy Statement

Employees will maintain high standards of dignity, respect and positive regard for one another. The Company adopts a zero-tolerance attitude against any kind of Sexual Harassment or discrimination. Allegations of sexual harassment will be dealt with seriously, expeditiously, sensitively and with confidentiality. Employees will be protected against victimization or retaliation for filing or reporting a complaint, and will also be protected from false accusations.

## 4.0 Scope

The policy extends to all staff members of Coforge Limited and its subsidiaries in India or across. This policy is deemed to be incorporated in the service conditions of all staff members.

## 5.0 Definitions

- **Staff Member** includes all staff members of Coforge Limited and its subsidiaries, and a client, vendor, or contractor in company premises, or elsewhere in India or abroad, during their tenure.
- **Respondent** is the staff member against whom the complaint has been filed, irrespective of gender.
- **Complainant** is the aggrieved person who files the complaint, irrespective of gender.
- **Sexual Harassment Redressal Committee (SHRC)** is the committee that addresses a complaint of sexual harassment.
- **Workplace** includes work premises and any place visited by a staff member arising out of or during the course of employment, including transportation provided by the employer.

## 7.2 Sexual Harassment Redressal Committee (SHRC)

SHRC shall be constituted at each location with a minimum of 4 members headed by a senior woman staff member (the Presiding Officer), with minimum 50% women and 1 external member. The term of office of the members of the SHRC shall be for a period of three (3) years.

The Sexual Harassment Redressal Committee email id is **shrc@coforge.com**.

If a committee member is the manager or supervisor of the complainant or the respondent, or is closely associated with the incident, that member shall not act on that matter.

## 8.0 Procedure to Complain

If any employee believes that he or she has been subjected to sexual harassment, the complaint should be reported in writing, with supporting documents and names and addresses of witnesses, to the SHRC **within three months** from the date of last occurrence of the alleged incident at **shrc@coforge.com**. If the complaint is made orally, the complainant will be requested to confirm it in writing. In an exceptional case, the time limit of three months may be relaxed by another three months if the SHRC is convinced of the reason for delay.

On receipt of the complaint, the SHRC shall share a copy with the respondent within **seven working days**. The respondent shall file a reply within **ten working days** from receipt of the complaint.

On the request of the complainant, the Committee may explore conciliation. No monetary settlement shall be made as a basis of conciliation.

## 10.0 Enquiry

Each complaint should be resolved within a period of **90 days** of the complaint. A minimum of three members of the Committee including the Presiding Officer shall be present during the inquiry. Any complaint that comes up has to be sent to the Internal Complaints Committee irrespective of the nature it carries; no preliminary enquiry is to be done here.

## 11.0 Action During Pendency of Inquiry

Upon a written request by the complainant, the Committee may recommend transfer of the complainant or respondent, or grant leave to the complainant up to a period of **three months**. Such leave is in addition to leave under the service rules.

## 14.0 Appeal

Any person aggrieved from the recommendations made by the Committee, or non-implementation of such recommendations, may appeal to the appellate authority notified under the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946.

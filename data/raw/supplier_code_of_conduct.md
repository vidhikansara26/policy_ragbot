# Supplier Code of Conduct

Source: [Coforge Supplier Code of Conduct](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/esg/Coforge_%20Supplier%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20VF.pdf)
Last review: 1 April 2025. Review date: 10 March 2026.

## Introduction

The Supplier Code of Conduct (SCoC) pertains to all suppliers, including Tier-2 and Tier-3 suppliers, subcontractors and labour contractors, vendors, contractors, and companies that supply products and services to Coforge. Coforge expects suppliers to embrace integrity and ethics in every business interaction.

## Labor Management and Human Rights

As highlighted in the Human Rights Policy, Coforge extends human rights commitments to suppliers. Suppliers must:

- Comply with applicable employment, benefits, health and safety, and anti-discrimination laws.
- Maintain written labor/workplace management policies and ensure subcontractors (Tier-2 and Tier-3) maintain similar policies.
- Provide a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, or bullying.
- Ensure equal treatment in salaries, recruitment, training, promotion, and working conditions.
- Comply with all applicable wage laws, including minimum wages, overtime, equal remuneration, and **timely disbursement of wages**.
- Prohibit child labor and refrain from forced or involuntary labor, human trafficking, and slavery. See the Coforge Modern Slavery Act 2015 statement.
- Offer fair compensation and working conditions, including **adequate rest periods and parental leave** that align with prevailing standards, and implement a grievance redressal mechanism with non-retaliation protection.
- Respect freedom of association and collective bargaining in accordance with applicable laws.
- Adhere to prevailing regulations concerning **working hours, rest days, overtime allowances, leaves, and other entitlements**.

This code does not publish a numeric Coforge employee Privilege Leave (PTO) entitlement. Leave entitlements for Coforge employees follow local law and service conditions, not a supplier-schedule day count.

## Occupational Health and Safety

Suppliers shall establish occupational health and safety controls, identify and mitigate workplace hazards, maintain written safety policies, and train all employees. OHS expectations apply to supplier employees, contract workers, on-site vendor personnel, and subcontractors.

## Ethical business dealing

Suppliers must comply with anti-corruption and anti-bribery laws including the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 (India) and the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA). Staff should be trained annually on anti-bribery and anti-corruption policies. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Suppliers who become aware of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information relating to Coforge shall not disclose it or trade in Coforge securities.

## Environmental sustainability and onboarding

Suppliers should set quantitative environmental targets and are preferred if they disclose environmental performance or hold ISO 14001 certification. Onboarding runs through Coforge's Vendor Portal and includes ESG risk screening covering modern slavery, labour rights, corruption, and environmental risks.

# Whistleblower Policy (version 1.5)

Source: [Coforge Whistleblower Policy](https://investors.coforge.com/hubfs/Whistleblower-Policy-1.pdf)

Version history includes a 2014 baseline, regulatory updates in 2022 and 2023, an annual review with no changes on 02-May-2024, and version 1.5 released 16-Jun-2026.

## 1.0 Context

Coforge Limited's Code requires Directors, officers and Staff Members to observe high standards of business and personal ethics. The Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI Listing Regulations require every listed company to establish a vigil mechanism with safeguards against victimization and direct access to the Chairman of the Audit Committee in exceptional cases.

## 2.0 Objective

The purpose of this Policy is to enable a person who observes an unethical practice or non-compliance to approach the Company without necessarily informing their supervisors and without revealing their identity if they so choose. Genuine whistleblowers are accorded complete protection from unfair treatment.

## 4.0 Scope

This Policy covers all Employees, Directors, officers and other stakeholders of the Company, including vendors, contractors, subcontractors, consultants, trainees, shareholders, former employees, job applicants, and any other third parties who have knowledge of a suspected violation.

Sexual harassment, discrimination, appraisal dissatisfaction, and personal service-condition complaints are **out of scope** and follow separate grievance mechanisms (including the POSH / SHRC process).

## 8.0 Reporting a Concern

Whistleblowers should use whichever channel they are most comfortable with:

1. **Dedicated Whistleblower Channel (Primary):** whistleblower@coforge.com (role-based inbox, monitored by the Compliance Officer).
2. **Compliance Officer:** complianceofficer@coforge.com. Internal courier: The Compliance Officer, Plot No. 2 & 2A, Sector Tech Zone, Yamuna Expressway, Greater Noida-201308.
3. **Chief People Officer** (if the concern involves the Compliance Officer): courier to Chief People Officer, Coforge Limited, at the Greater Noida address above.
4. **Chairman of the Audit Committee** (exceptional cases): courier to The Chairman of Audit Committee at the same Greater Noida address.

The concern should preferably be reported in writing via e-mail in English, or by phone/orally. The complaint can be raised in any language; investigation minutes are maintained in English. Anonymous complaints may be acted on where they contain specific, verifiable allegations. Where contact details are provided, the Compliance Officer shall acknowledge receipt within **5 working days**.

## 9.0 Compliance Committee

The CEO constitutes a Compliance Committee of at least three members. At least one of the Compliance Officer or CFO must be present. The first meeting shall be held within **7 working days** of the concern being reported. Complaints involving the CEO, CFO, or a Compliance Committee member bypass the committee and go directly to the Chairman of the Audit Committee.
